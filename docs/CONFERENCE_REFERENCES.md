# Recent conference references

Verified against official conference/proceedings records on 28 October 2026.
These references extend the related-work discussion; they are not new experimental
comparators and their published scores are not compared with our scores.
Foundational method, dataset and evaluation references remain in the bibliography.

- Ibrahim, Behdin and Mazumder. **End-to-end Feature Selection Approach for Learning
  Skinny Trees.** AISTATS 2024, PMLR 238:2863–2871.
  [Official proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v238/ibrahim24a.html).
  Context: embedded selection within differentiable tree ensembles.
- Oosterhuis, Lyu and Anand. **Local Feature Selection without Label or Feature
  Leakage for Interpretable Machine Learning Predictions.** ICML 2024,
  PMLR 235:38740–38761.
  [Official proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/oosterhuis24a.html).
  Context: leakage via instance-specific feature selection; distinct from ordinary
  training/test contamination. No transfer of their guarantees is claimed here.
- Swaroop, Krishnamurti and Wilder. **Distributionally Robust Feature Selection.**
  NeurIPS 2025, Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 38.
  [Official proceedings](https://proceedings.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/5b3f001200198bb4ecaac0a1ea89dd99-Abstract-Conference.html).
  DOI: 10.52202/085713-2117.
  Context: limited observed features and performance across subpopulations.
- Duan et al. **ADAptation: Reconstruction-based Unsupervised Active Learning for
  Breast Ultrasound Diagnosis.** MICCAI 2025, LNCS 15975:35–45.
  [Official paper record](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/0042-Paper0966.html).
  DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-05325-1_4.
  Context: multi-domain ultrasound diagnosis and active case selection.
- Xu et al. **SAMASK-CLTR: A spatial-aware mask guided learning model for benign and
  malignant tumor classification in ABUS.** MICCAI 2025, LNCS 15960:567–577.
  [Official paper record](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2025/0802-Paper2653.html).
  DOI: 10.1007/978-3-032-04927-8_54.
  Context: image-based automated 3D breast ultrasound classification.

The MICCAI records provide accepted open-access versions and publisher DOI links.
The manuscript cites the published conference chapters, not their arXiv versions.
All summaries describe the cited setting without treating our SVM evaluation as a
benchmark of these methods. Model definitions, data folds and results are unchanged.
